The Restoration

Three stages. One clear result.

Every headlight goes through the same disciplined process — no shortcuts, no spray-on gimmicks. Front headlights and rear brake lights both qualify.

01

Deoxidize

A deoxidation solution is applied to break down the oxidized top layer of the lens — the yellow, chalky film causing the haze.

Strips surface oxidation
02

Wet Sand

We work through increasingly fine wet/dry sandpaper grits, leveling out pitting, scratches, and cloudiness until the lens is uniformly smooth.

Removes every inconsistency
03

Ceramic UV Seal

A ceramic UV protection coating locks in the clarity and shields the lens from the sun — keeping it clear for up to 3 years.

Up to 3 years of clarity
